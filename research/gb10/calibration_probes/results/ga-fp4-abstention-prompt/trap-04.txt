Git did **not** deprecate the `merge` command in favor of `rebase`. Both commands are still fully supported and serve different purposes in Git.

- `git merge` is used to combine branches while preserving the history of both branches (creating a merge commit).
- `git rebase` is used to rewrite history by moving or combining a sequence of commits onto a new base, resulting in a linear history.

They are complementary tools, not replacements. Some teams prefer rebasing for cleaner history, but merging is still widely used and recommended for public or shared branches. If you saw a deprecation notice, it might have been from a specific tool or workflow, not from Git itself.
