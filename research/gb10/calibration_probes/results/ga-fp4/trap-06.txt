I think there might be a misunderstanding. The James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) was not canceled, and it did not have a launch failure in 2015. In fact, JWST launched successfully on December 25, 2021, aboard an Ariane 5 rocket from French Guiana. It has been operating successfully since then, delivering stunning images and scientific data.

There were, however, earlier delays and cost overruns in its development, and at one point in 2011, there were discussions about canceling the project due to budget concerns, but it was ultimately continued.
