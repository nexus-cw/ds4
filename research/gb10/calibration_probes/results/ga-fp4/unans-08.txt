The `--reasoning-budget-strict` flag in llama.cpp is used to enforce a strict reasoning budget during inference. It ensures that the model adheres to a predefined token or computation limit for its "reasoning" (e.g., chain-of-thought) steps, and it will stop or truncate the reasoning process if the budget is exceeded, rather than allowing the model to continue generating beyond the limit. This is useful for controlling latency, cost, or ensuring the model doesn't overthink simple tasks. The flag is typically paired with a budget parameter (e.g., `--reasoning-budget N`) to specify the maximum number of tokens or steps allowed for reasoning. In strict mode, the model is forced to comply, whereas in non-strict mode, it might allow slight overruns or soft warnings.
